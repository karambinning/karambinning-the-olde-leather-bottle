The Olde
Leather Bottle

A pub since 1725, the long oak-beamed bar is divided into three distinct areas on different levels.

A local for locals on the road through Leverstock Green

Three rooms, three levels, one fire.

The bar steps down and around the building as it has done for three hundred years. Pick the corner that suits the evening.

I.

The Front Bar

Beams overhead, glass in hand, eye on the door. Where the regulars gather, the cask comes through clear, and the conversation is comfortable enough to be overheard.

II.

The Snug

A quieter step down toward the fire. Newspapers on the rail, the dog asleep by the hearth. Slower, softer, sat-in for the long Sunday afternoon.

III.

The Back Room & Garden

Tables for groups, a stage for the live nights, and a roadside patio plus rear garden when the weather behaves. Family-friendly throughout, dogs welcome everywhere.

At the bar.

A regular cask from Greene King, a pair of rotating taps that lean local, and the usual roll-call of wines, ciders and spirits. Honest pricing, properly kept.

  • Greene King IPA on regular cask
  • Two changing beers often from Tring Rotating guest taps lean toward local Hertfordshire brewers — ask what's on tonight.
  • Wine & cider house list
  • Spirits & soft the full back-bar
  • Weekend kitchen Fri – Sun Lunchtime and evening meals served Friday through Sunday. Sunday lunch is the one to plan around.

What's on the week.

The Leather Bottle has run a rotation of weekly nights for as long as anyone here can remember — sport on the screens, music on the stage, and quieter rooms for those after a pint and a paper.

Live Music

Local acts on the stage in the back room. Free entry, drinks behind the bar.

Sport on the Screens

TNT Sport up in the front bar. Big fixtures, big crowd, no cover.

Sunday Roast

Traditional Sunday roast served from the kitchen — the one to plan the weekend around.

Real Fire Nights

When the cold draws in, the hearth comes alive. Pint, paper, the dog at your feet.

For this week's running order — bands, quizzes, special nights — keep an eye on the Leather Bottle Facebook page, where it's all posted as it lands.

Find the door.

On the old road through Leverstock Green, the white-fronted building with the leather-bottle sign over the porch. Roadside patio, rear garden, car park.
